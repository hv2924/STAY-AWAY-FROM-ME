Cleaning & Mess Drag

  • Dirt is only collected when your motorized floor nozzle passes within its intake radius — get close, then watch crumbs magnetically rip into the canister.
  • Rolling over heavy messes (cereal, glitter, mud, coffee, metal shavings) drags your speed down to 62–78%. Break off a thick pile if a bot closes in — finish it later.
  • At 90% clean, remaining patches shimmer gold every 3 seconds. At 98.5% a final suction burst vacuums the last stray specks automatically.

Outrunning A.I.R.O. Units

  • Bots patrol at 80% and chase at 88% of your clean speed — in a straight line on clean floor, they can never catch you. You only get caught by cornering yourself, crashing head-on into furniture, or getting greedy in heavy mess.
  • Break line-of-sight through doorways and between chair legs. Once a bot loses sight and distance, it drops back to patrolling.
  • Lure bots onto traps: shag rugs, bath mats, tangled cables, train tracks. They jam for 4.5 seconds with strobing amber hazard lights.
  • Weave past a bot within 0.65 m without touching it for a NEAR MISS: a static spark and a 0.6 s adrenaline boost of +12%.
  • Head-on crashes stall you at 35% speed for 0.45 s — shallow-angle scrapes let you slide along surfaces.

Reading the HUD

  • DIRT-O-METER (top-left): canister fill and, past 90%, a remaining-patch counter.
  • Stopwatch (top-right): your run timer and stored personal best.
  • Acoustic Radar (bottom-center): pulses red toward off-camera bots you can hear but not see. Brown vignette = mess drag. Red heartbeat = a bot within 2.0 m.
FIELD NOTE: your Mk-IV is fully cordless — no battery meter, no tether. Your only resources are routing, momentum and nerve. Good luck, human.
